A great performative boss (philosopher) once told me:

"The trail that matcha leaves behind is one you shall follow to atain enlightenment. Savings shall be lost, and individualism, a past-time. Following those who performative-maxx are those who understand the world."

And that I shall...

I started my Matcha Trail in late 2024 as I was convinced to try the green drink at one of the temples of entry, Cafe Maiko. I was hesitant at first. "Why would someone drink something that originates from ground up leaves?" I asked myself. However, I didn't know it then that my entire personality would change and the course of my life would shift for the better. 

Walking up to the small square kiosk, I saw what would only be described as a performative male's wet dream, over 10 styles of matcha and hojicha. Scrolling down, the drink stared back into my un-enlightened soul. "Drink me" yelled the 3-tiered strawberry matcha latte. "No, drink me", the oat milk matcha whispered. Swiping upward from the bottom of the page to the top, I continued to scroll down until my finger landed on the fabled "whole milk matcha latte". Selecting it, it appeared into the cart, "Added." Fear struck me as I held onto my credit card, I was about to lose my matcha-ginity. I tapped my credit card on the screen as $7 left my bank account. I couldn't turn back.  

Standing to the side, I waited as the baristas made the drink. The 12 ounce plastic cup with Cafe Maiko stamped on it was filled to the brim with whole, white milk. As the milk hit the bottom of the cup, my mouth salivated. Then came the matcha, as they help up a squeeze bottle filled with the heavenly green matcha. Squeezing it into the cup, the matcha mixture flowed into the cup. After mixing the fluids in the cup, it was finished.

My name was called as I picked up the drink. Holding the cup up to my mouth, the smooth green, milky drink filled my mouth. *Boom...* What was once pure milk had been liberated to become one with the savior, matcha powder. It was no longer plain tasting milk, but one filled with grassy, bitter after notes. "So this was matcha. This is what people were raving about" I thought... "It's meh."

Maybe I don't understand it. Maybe the barista had an off day. Maybe the milk was spoiled. These thoughts filled my mind as I continued to drink. This couldn't be where it ends. There has to be a light at the end of the tunnel called performative-maxxing. I need to attain that. 

So, I set my path onward to reach this. This treacherous path is not an easy one (especially for my wallet), but it must be done. I must become one with the performative.
